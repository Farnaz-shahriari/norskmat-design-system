# Brand context

Sources, stiftelsennorskmat.no, ksl.no, nytnorge.no, checked August 2026.

## The organization

Stiftelsen Norsk Mat is <cite index="2-1">an independent foundation that contributes to greater diversity, quality, and value creation in Norwegian food production</cite>. It <cite index="2-1">manages three labeling schemes that give consumers confidence and information about the origin and quality of food, Nyt Norge, Beskyttede Betegnelser, and Spesialitet</cite>, and it also <cite index="2-1">runs the quality system for agriculture called KSL, approves farms for the Inn på tunet scheme, gives consumers fact based information on organic production, and runs Lokalmat.no, the largest Norwegian portal for local food and drink producers</cite>. Based in Oslo, <cite index="3-1">it has 18 employees split into a quality department and a communications department</cite>.

## The platforms, one organization, many faces

Public sites confirm several distinct platforms under the same parent, each with its own audience.

* Nyt Norge, consumer facing. <cite index="5-1">The official origin label for Norwegian food and drink, its aim is to make it easy to choose Norwegian in the store</cite>. Tone is warm, everyday, proud of local food and the people behind it.
* KSL, farmer facing. <cite index="4-1">Kvalitetssystem i landbruket, the farmer's own tool for internal control and quality assurance, letting the farmer check that operations follow Norwegian laws, regulations, and industry requirements</cite>. Tone is practical, professional, closer to a work tool than a consumer brand.
* Lokalmat.no, marketplace facing, portal for local food and drink producers, connecting producers and buyers.
* Spesialitet and Beskyttede Betegnelser, certification facing, for producers with a distinct or protected product.
* Stiftelsen Norsk Mat itself, institutional, the umbrella voice, press, annual reports, governance.

## What this means for the design system

Same underlying institution, same values, quality, trust, Norwegian origin, but each platform speaks to a different person, shopper, farmer, producer, journalist, so color, tone, and density of information can differ while structure stays shared.
